Greg Jones Cashflow Dashboard — v4
All four account tabs
==================================

Supersedes all earlier zips.

WHAT CHANGED IN v4
------------------
Only the "Business Working Account" tab was ever being fetched. The other
three account cards — Regulation, Savings, Profit Reinvestment — were showing
HARDCODED placeholder figures ($7,059 / $50,000 / $0) that looked exactly like
live data, and the "Total Across All Accounts" tile added them up as if real.

Now all four tabs are read:

  Business Working Account        the weekly series          (required)
  Regulation Bank Account         opening + closing balance
  Business Savings Account        opening + closing balance
  Profit Reinvest. Bank Account   opening + closing balance

If a tab cannot be read, its card shows a dash and names the tab to check. It
is excluded from the total, and the total says how many accounts are missing.
No placeholder is ever shown as though it were a real balance.

The Connect tab now lists all four tabs with their read status ("read" /
"not read"), so you can verify at a glance.

Balances refresh every 60 seconds; the weekly figures still refresh every 5.


CARRIED OVER FROM v3
--------------------
- Rows matched by their LABEL in column B, not by row number.
- Dashboard opens on the most recent week that HAS figures.
- Year-to-date falls back to the most recent year holding data.
- Connect tab shows which row each figure was read from.


HOW TO APPLY
------------
1. Unzip into the ROOT of your repo, overwriting.
2. rm APPLY.txt
3. git add -A
   git commit -m "Read all four account tabs; row matching by label"
   git push -u origin claude/client-files-review-g1ngpj
4. Netlify redeploys automatically.


HOW TO VERIFY
-------------
Open the Connect tab after reloading. You should see:

  ACCOUNT TABS READ — 4 of 4
    Business Working Account         read
    Regulation Bank Account          read
    Business Savings Account         read
    Profit Reinvest. Bank Account    read

  ROWS READ FROM THE SHEET
    Opening Bank Balance   row 8      (green = matched by label)
    Total Receipts         row 76
    ...

Anything amber means it could not be matched — send me that panel.
