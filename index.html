Connect a Google Sheet
Paste the link to your cashflow spreadsheet. The dashboard reads it and keeps itself up to date as you edit.
Before you connect
1. Share the sheet. In Google Sheets choose Share → General access → Anyone with the link → Viewer. Without this the dashboard cannot read it.

2. Check the tab name. The weekly figures are read from a tab called Business Working Account. Rows are found by the label in their left-hand column — Week Commencing, Opening Bank Balance, Total Business Receipts, Total Business Payments, Closing Bank Balance — so rows can be added or moved without breaking anything. After connecting, the panel above lists exactly which row supplied each figure.

3. Updates. Edits appear within about 5 seconds while this tab is open. For instant updates, install the Apps Script bridge on the sheet (see README.md) and paste its /exec link here instead — that switches the dashboard to a live websocket.
